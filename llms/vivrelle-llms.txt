## Products

Vivrelle sells consumer memberships, priced in USD and billed monthly. Prices below are
quoted from the Vivrelle FAQ.

- Classique — $139/month, one item at a time, retail value up to $4,000
- Couture — $239/month, one item at a time, full closet including limited editions
- Classique+ — $249/month, two items at a time from the Classique closet
- Couture+ — $339/month, two items at a time across the Couture and Classique closets
- Premier (add-on) — $49/month, one extra item per month, jewelry up to $1,000 retail
- Réservé (add-on, invite-only) — $800/month, ultra high-end closet

Membership terms: 30-day minimum hold per item; cancellation permitted after the first
three months once the item is returned and received; membership may be frozen for
$10/month.
